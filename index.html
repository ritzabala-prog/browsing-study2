How cities are rethinking public transport for the next decade

Across Europe, planners are experimenting with new ways of moving people through crowded centres, from low-emission zones to on-demand shuttles.

Experts say the biggest challenge is not technology but habit: commuters tend to stick to routines even when better options exist, and small changes in design can shift behaviour at scale.

Pilot projects in several cities show promising early results, although long-term effects remain to be seen. Officials plan to publish a full evaluation next year.

Critics argue that investment should focus on affordability and reliability before anything else, a view shared by many riders surveyed in recent studies.
